What happened?

  • ⚠ You followed a QR code without first verifying its source.
  • ⚠ You were asked to provide personal information before confirming who operated the website.
  • ⚠ The giveaway used excitement, prizes, and a deadline to encourage quick action.

How can you protect yourself?

  • Confirm who posted or sent a QR code before scanning it.
  • Preview and inspect the website address.
  • Research unexpected contests and giveaways.
  • Be cautious when a site requests personal information.
  • Do not rely only on professional-looking colors or logos.
  • Remember that legitimate-looking websites can still be fake.
